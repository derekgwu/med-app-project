Audiology Clinic

Services

  • Diagnostic hearing assessments
  • Hearing Aid fittings
  • Vestibular/balance assessments
  • Tinnitus counselling
  • Cerumen (ear wax) management
  • Outreach to rural areas
  • Mobile services available for hospital inpatients

Who we see

Clients of all ages – newborn to geriatric

Location

School for the Deaf

Audiology refers to closest schools in Blantyre region are Maryview and MountainView.*


Diagnosis of a significant hearing loss does not guarantee admittance to any Deaf School as vacancies are limited.
