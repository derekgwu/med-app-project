Cotrimoxazole Preventive Therapy

Exposed children should be referred to COT clinic for initiation of CPT and further testing. Exposed children remain on CPT until confirmed negative at 24 months of age or older when confirming HIV negative 6 weeks after stopping breast-feeding. Continue CPT for life for all HIV positive patients.

Dosage of Cotrimoxazole (CPT) Preventive Therapy

Age Group 480mg tablets
6 weeks - 5 months ¼ tablet 24-hourly (120 mg)
6 months - 4 years ½ tablet 24-hourly (240 mg)
5 - 14 years 1 tablet 24-hourly (480 mg)
15 years and above 1 tablet 12-hourly (960 mg)
