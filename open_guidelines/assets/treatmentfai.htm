Treatment failure

Suspect treatment failure if all of the following clinical conditions are met:
  • On ART for 12 month or more
  • Currently good adherence (but may have had poor adherence in the past)
  • New WHO clinical stage 3 or 4 condition

Investigations

Do CD4 and VL
- must have VL to confirm failure

  • If VL 1,000 copies/ml: continue to support intensive adherence

  • If VL ≥1,000 copies/ml: Treatment failure confirmed, refer to ART provider for management.
