Ophthalmology

What we do

  • Vision testing
  • Fundoscopy. Direct fundoscopy for suspected raised intracranial pressure or cerebral malaria can be performed in the paediatric wrads. More detailed indirect fundoscopy in the ophthalmology department.
  • B Scan
  • Anterior and Posterior segment OCT
  • Visual Fields (Perimetry)
  • Pachymetry
  • Fundus autofluorescence
  • Anterior segment pictures
  • Amblyoscopy (paeds autorefractor)
  • Fundus Pictures (on request)
  • Interpretation (report)
  • Laser surgery (YAG laser iridotomy (LPI – Laser Peripheral iridotomy), YAG laser, capsulotomy, Argon Laser Trabeculoplasty (ALT), Diode Laser)

Referral pathway

We have a registrar on call everyday with consultant support. If a stable patient needs review, please write a consultation request and leave it with the clerk and he will be seen on the same day (when consultation form is sent early in the morning). When the consultation form is sent late in the afternoon, the patient will be seen next day.
For emergencies/urgent reviews, please call the eye department ward extension, or call the registrar's line via 0111578741, or contact any registrar on mobile to send the message to the colleague on call. Whatsapp messages are not a good mode of communication in case of emergency.
