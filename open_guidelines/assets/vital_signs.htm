Physiological Vital Signs

Respiratory Rates (count for ONE WHOLE minute in children < 1 year!)

AGE RESPIRATORY RATE
Newborn Up to 60/min
Under 1 year 30 - 40/min
1-5 years 25 - 35/min
5-12 years 20 - 25/min
Over 12 years 15 - 20/min

Heart Rates (NEVER TRUST THE SAT MONITOR - FEEL THE PULSE!)

AGE HEART RATE
Newborn 110 - 180/min
Under 1 year 110 - 160/min
1-5 years 95 - 150/min
5-12 years 80 - 120/min
Over 12 years 60 - 100/min

Blood Pressure (cuff size 2/3 of arm/calf - leg pressures in lying patient!)

AGE SYSTOLIC BP
Newborn 50 - 70 mm Hg
Under 1 year70 - 90 mm Hg
1-5 years 80 - 100 mm Hg
5-12 years 90 - 110 mm Hg
Over 12 years 100 - 120 mm Hg

Weight estimation (from 1 year to puberty around 13 years)

(Age + 4) x 2 or 3 x age + 7
